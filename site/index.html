UNIZY · ECON0013 · ANSWER BOOKLET
Q3 · Explain why a profit-maximising monopolist produces on the elastic part of demand
A monopolist maximises profit where MR = MC. Since MC > 0, MR must be positive, so |ε| > 1 and demand is elastic at the chosen output. On the inelastic part, cutting output would raise revenue and lower costs, so no monopolist stays there.
