What's the difference between an offer and an invitation to treat?
An offer is a definite promise to be bound on set terms as soon as it's accepted. An invitation to treat only invites people to make offers — goods on a shop shelf are the classic example (Pharmaceutical Society v Boots, 1953). You make the offer at the till; the shop accepts.
