Review rules

Who can leave a review?

Anyone with a Unizy account who has started the free 7-day trial: confirm your email, open Unizy and set up your university and course. You don’t have to use the tutor. Each account, and so each email address, can leave one review, and you can edit or delete it any time. The Unizy team can’t review Unizy.

Do you remove reviews?

Only reviews that break the rules: abuse or hate, spam or links, other people’s personal information, or reviews that aren’t about Unizy. We never remove or hide a review because it’s negative, and we don’t edit what you write.

What’s shown with my review?

Your first name as it appears on Unizy, the date, and (if you choose) your university, course and year. Your email address is never shown.

Are reviews paid or incentivised?

No. We don’t pay for reviews or offer rewards for them, and we don’t write reviews ourselves.
