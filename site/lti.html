unizy

Unizy in your Moodle, Canvas or Blackboard

Unizy is a study companion for UK university students: notes, flashcards, practice questions and study rooms built around each student’s exact modules. With this LTI 1.3 connection, students open Unizy from a link in their course page and arrive already signed in with their university account, verified as students at your university, with that course’s module open.

What Unizy receives

Only what a standard LTI 1.3 resource-link launch carries: the student’s name and university email (needed to sign them in), their course’s title and short code (to open the right module) and their role. Unizy doesn’t use grade passback, roster or deep-linking services, and never writes anything to your LMS. See the privacy policy.

Setting it up

Moodle (Site administration → Plugins → Activity modules → External tool → Manage tools → configure a tool manually): enter the URLs above, set Public key type to “Keyset URL”, set “Share launcher’s name” and “Share launcher’s email” to Always, and default launch container to “New window”. Save, then open “View configuration details” and send us the Platform ID (issuer), Client ID, Deployment ID, Authentication request URL and Public keyset URL.

Canvas (Admin → Developer keys → + LTI key): choose “Enter URL” and paste the JSON configuration URL, then turn the key on and add the app to your account or sub-account with its Client ID. Send us the Client ID and Deployment ID. The issuer is usually https://canvas.instructure.com.

Blackboard Learn (via the Anthology Developer Portal): register an LTI 1.3 tool with the URLs above, then add it under LTI Tool Providers → Register LTI 1.3/Advantage Tool with its Client ID. Send us the Issuer, Client ID, Deployment ID, OIDC auth request endpoint and JWKS URL.

Email the details to hello@unizy.uk and we’ll switch your university on, usually within a day.

For students

If your course page doesn’t have a Unizy link yet, you can still sign up at unizy.uk, verify your uni email, connect your Moodle, Canvas or Blackboard calendar and upload your timetable from the Plan page.
