Example MSIN0023 question

A retailer applies a discount to orders: 5% if the total exceeds £100, 10% if it exceeds £500, otherwise none. (a) Write Python-style pseudocode for a function returning the final price. (b) State two test cases that check the boundaries. (c) Explain why the order of your conditions matters. [20 marks]
